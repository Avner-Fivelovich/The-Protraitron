The PORTRAITRON 3000

Avner Fivelovich, Hila Regev Shabath, Shira Shmuely

Description & Functionality

Our latest invention shall capture a person standing in a VERY specific spot! The drawing is executed on paper using our patented MARKERTRON, then struck with a commemorative stamp of OUR OWN FACES, and finally handed over to the subject before they're doomed!

Sketch from Image

  • CAPTURE high-definition picture of the subject (3s after keyboard click)
  • PROCESS Camera input to strokes
  • OPTIMIZES strokes order algorithm

Draw

  • PICK UP Marker
  • DRAW strokes on page (main goal- our proof of concept)
  • LET GO of marker
  • PICK UP Stamp
  • STAMP the page
  • LET GO of stamp

Hand The Drawing (Nice to Have)

  • GET the page
  • HAND the page to a designated location

Proof of Concept

The PORTRAITRON will be able to draw a circle on paper, at this point the vectors are predetermained by a function (rather then by the camera input), and represented in the same format as the output of the image to vector algorithm we intend to use: https://swiftsketch.github.io/
