Mechanical Specification Sheet

Robotiq 2F-85 Grasped Pen Mount (Refined)

Design Strategy & Grip Configurations

To eliminate tool-swap latencies and electrical harness modifications on the physical UR5e flange, the Portraitron employs a grasped tool strategy.

The 3D-printed marker holder features an integrated top Pinch Block that is grabbed directly by the robot's active Robotiq 2F-85 adaptive gripper. The bottom of the block houses an integrated compliance sleeve slider enclosing the Staedtler Lumocolor drawing marker.

The laboratory has verified the availability of two physical gripper hands:

  • Hand 23 (S/N C-47150): Robotiq 2F adaptive gripper, standard flat silicone fingertip pads.
  • Hand 24 (S/N C-48685): Robotiq 2F adaptive gripper, standard flat silicone fingertip pads.
🔒 Mechanical Anti-Rotation Interlocking

To completely eliminate tool twist or slide during drawing, we model custom recessed anti-rotation pockets on the left and right sides of the pinch block. The gripper's $22\text{mm} \times 37.5\text{mm}$ flat rubber fingers slide snugly into these channels, locking the tool firmly against lateral and rotational forces.

Multi-Model Paradigm (Wrist Throat vs. Fast Print)

Based on user feedback and mechanical stability considerations, the CAD model has been branched into two variations. This ensures a fast fall-back contingency if printing overnight is not possible, alongside a robust production-grade model.

🔥 Robust & Stable Model

Ultimate Stability: The core block is lengthened to 100 mm so it extends deep into the gripper throat towards the wrist. By tunneling the pen slot up to 70 mm depth, the pen's center of mass resides directly between the finger pad centers, eliminating bending moments.

Total Height 100.0 mm
Pen Tunnel Depth 70.0 mm
Solid Ceiling Stop 30.0 mm (3.0 cm)
Core Pinch Width 26.0 mm (hides cylinder)

⚡ Fast-to-Print Model

Rapid Contingency: A downscaled, lightweight 42 mm tall block. Ideal for quick contingency printing (under 1.5 - 2 hours) while maintaining standard pocket channels and the solid 30mm ceiling stop.

Total Height 42.0 mm
Pen Tunnel Depth 12.0 mm
Solid Ceiling Stop 30.0 mm
Core Pinch Width 22.0 mm
  • Smooth Z-Axis Entry (No Top Lip): Both models have vertical side locking channels that are completely **open at the top**. This enables the robot's closed gripper to slide straight down in Z onto the tool on its holder, without hitting a top shoulder flange.
  • 3.0 cm Solid Material Ceiling: To prevent the pen from pushing upwards out of calibration during drawings, both models maintain exactly **30.0 mm of solid structural material** above the hollow slide sleeve to serve as a robust ceiling stop.

Mating Recesses Tolerances: Recess pockets are oversized by 0.5 mm in width to allow the standard rubber fingers to slide into the slots comfortably without sanding, while preventing lateral play.

Staedtler Lumocolor 317 M & Compliance Slider

The universal permanent medium marker used for physical ink testing has a snug 10.0 mm barrel diameter.

To absorb table slants and to prevent crushing the pen tip on initial engagement, a soft mechanical spring is nested concentric to the marker cylinder sleeve:

  • Linear Stroke Play: Design a 12.0 mm vertical stroke clearance using guide-pin channels on the outer slide-cage.
  • Travel Stop Guide: Use a single M3 machine screw passing through the outer cage guide-slot into the inner slider to act as a physical stop.
  • Compression Spring: Select a soft, wire-wound spring from the workbench (wire: $0.6\text{--}0.8\,\text{mm}$, OD: $14\text{--}15\,\text{mm}$, length: $35\,\text{mm}$) to provide a light restorative downward force.
