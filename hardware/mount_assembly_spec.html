Mechanical Specification Sheet

Robotiq 2F-85 Grasped Pen Mount (Refined)

Design Strategy Overview

To eliminate mechanical tool-swap times and prevent unbolting wiring assemblies on the physical UR5e flange, the Portraitron utilizes a grasped tool strategy.

The 3D-printed marker holder features an integrated top Pinch Block that is actively grabbed by the robot's Robotiq 2F-85 adaptive gripper. The bottom of the block houses a custom spring-loaded slider sleeve containing the Staedtler marker.

🔒 Mechanical Interlocking Principle

To prevent the holder from twisting or sliding during draw moves, we model anti-rotation pockets matching the standard flat silicone pads of the Robotiq 2F-85 gripper. When the fingers close, the rubber pads slide into these pockets, creating a rigid mechanical lock.

Design Refinements (Top-Entry, 30mm Ceiling & Core Protrusion Fix)

Based on real-world kinematics and force-torque transmission physics on the UR5e wrist, three crucial design refinements have been implemented:

  • 1. Smooth Top Entry (Open Channel): The side locking pockets have **no top lip**—they extend all the way to the top of the block ($Z = 42.0\,\text{mm}$). This creates a smooth vertical channel so the robot's gripper fingers can slide straight down in Z onto the tool from above without colliding with a top flange.
  • 2. Solid 30mm Ceiling End-Stop: Instead of tunneling the circular pen hole all the way through the block, **we leave exactly 3.0 cm (30.0 mm) of solid material at the top** (Z from 12.0 to 42.0 mm). This creates a robust physical ceiling stop for the pen, preventing the pen from sliding upwards during drawing, while tunneling the lower 12.0 mm of the block.
  • 3. Extended 26mm Core Width (Cylinder Protrusion Fix): Since the outer sliding cylinder has a $24.0\,\text{mm}$ diameter, setting the core pinch width to $22.0\,\text{mm}$ caused the cylinder to protrude out of the sides, blocking the gripper fingers. By **enlarging the core pinch thickness to $26.0\,\text{mm}$ and overall width to $34.0\,\text{mm}$**, the cylinder is completely hidden inside the core web with a $1.0\,\text{mm}$ safety margin, providing a perfectly flat, clean pinch face!

Spring-Loaded Compliance Assembly

To absorb physical slants of the drawing table and to prevent crushing the pen tip on initial engagement, a soft mechanical spring is nested concentric to the marker cylinder sleeve:

  • Linear Stroke Play: Design a 12.0 mm vertical stroke clearance using guide-pin channels on the outer slide-cage.
  • Travel Stop Guide: Use a single M3 machine screw passing through the outer cage guide-slot into the inner slider to act as a physical stop.
  • Compression Spring: Select a soft, wire-wound spring from the workbench (wire: $0.6\text{--}0.8\,\text{mm}$, OD: $14\text{--}15\,\text{mm}$, length: $35\,\text{mm}$) to provide a light restorative downward force.
